# LeadLift — Sales playbook, version 1

## Ideal initial buyer
Owner or sales/operations leader of a solar installer or property maintenance business receiving repeat inbound enquiries from web forms, phone, email or WhatsApp. Target organisations where a new signed project justifies implementation fees. These are hypotheses, not evidence that specific companies have a problem.

## Offer
**Paid implementation:** From R25,000 scoped setup, with optional R7,500/month monitoring and iteration. Third-party charges excluded. For the first 2 pilot customers only, a narrower **R4,900 paid process audit** can be offered, credited toward implementation if both parties sign a subsequent scope; this does not include automation or a guarantee. Pilot rate is a proposed experiment, not a site-wide published price.

### Discovery, 15–20 minutes
1. What channels generate new enquiries? Which ones receive most volume?
2. Who owns a new enquiry and where is that documented?
3. What qualifies a lead for a technical/site assessment?
4. How are urgent cases handled out of hours?
5. What software and team permissions are currently available?
6. What does a successful workflow change look like in one month?
7. Is there a budget and an accountable decision-maker?

### Demo agenda
- Show an illustrative inbound lead and transparent rule-based qualification.
- Agree what context is missing.
- Map the human handoff, consent and owner.
- Explain that actual WhatsApp, inbox, calendar and CRM links are not connected without authorization.
- Offer a written, fixed-scope quote or the R4,900 process audit.

### Proposal structure
1. Client legal name and contact, date, scope and exclusions.
2. Current process and measurable baseline, validated by client.
3. Deliverables: intake mapping, qualification fields, workflow routes, approval/testing, owner training, 14-day defect window (if agreed).
4. Dependencies: permissions, connected accounts, third-party fees, data retention and documented consent handling.
5. Fixed fee, payment milestones, refund/termination rules and realistic timeline to be agreed.
6. Sign-off and clear after-sales support boundaries.
**Do not call this template an executed agreement or promise savings.**

### Follow-up after an opt-in or inbound request
Subject: Your enquiry workflow — next steps
Thanks for discussing your current lead response process. Based on what you shared, the main decision is whether to start with a fixed-scope audit or an implementation quote. If you're happy for me to send the outline, I'll include the exact deliverables, limitations and estimated cost. You can ask us to stop contacting you at any time.

### One-time consent request for *uncontacted* researched businesses
**This is a consent request, not a sales pitch. Send at most once to a recipient who has not refused, using a verified business identity and sender address.**
Subject: May LeadLift email you a brief business service outline?
Hello,
I am writing on behalf of LeadLift Revenue Systems to ask whether your business consents to receiving one email about an enquiry-handling workflow service. We would use this email address solely for that purpose if you agree. Please reply with explicit written consent if you wish to receive the outline. If not, we will not send it. You can withdraw consent or object at any time by replying to the sender. Our privacy notice is at https://leadlift-gt3rs.vercel.app/privacy.html.
Kind regards,
LeadLift Revenue Systems
[Verified legal operator identity and reply-to business email must be completed before use]

The 2025 POPIA Regulation 6 and Section 69 constrain first contact. Keep a record of the permission request and any refusal. Do not treat a public email address as consent. Do not automate repeated requests.

### Sales ethics and record keeping
- Do not claim a customer relationship, appointment, reply or payment unless evidence exists.
- Never contact a person marked refused or do_not_contact.
- Never buy or scrape bulk personal contact lists.
- Record outreach in `sales_activity` only after a platform confirms that it was actually sent.
