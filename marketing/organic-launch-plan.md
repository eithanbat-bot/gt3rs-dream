# Initial 3-week organic marketing programme

**This is a plan, not a claim that a post has been distributed.**

Week 1: Publish the LeadLift landing page, privacy notice, interactive demo and South African solar lead response checklist. Verify the request form against the Supabase backend. Prepare permission-compliant outreach workflow and a consent/request tracker. Do not use paid ads before a validated conversion flow.

Week 2: Share the solar enquiry checklist as an educational post on an authorized business account once a social connector is added. Post a short comparison of manual response vs structured qualification with no fabricated conversion statistics. Review new inbound requests, answer them with permission, invite suitable buyers to a discovery call. Only email researched prospects after valid consent or qualifying customer exception.

Week 3: Publish a facilities/property-service triage checklist, test two headline variants, and review counts of form views, submissions, genuine discovery calls and paid audits. Treat any initial sales narrative as hypothetical until backed by customer consent and observed outcomes.

KPI definitions:
- Researched accounts: verified website and industry, no consent implied.
- Consented accounts: explicit recorded consent and scope.
- Contacted accounts: send confirmed and logged.
- Inbound demo requests: successful backend insert and real consent timestamp.
- Proposals sent: real, permissioned proposals.
- Won: agreement signed and payment confirmed.
