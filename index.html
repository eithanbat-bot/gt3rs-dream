Common questions

Before we work together

Does LeadLift guarantee more sales?

No. The service aims to improve lead-handling consistency. Results depend on your traffic, offer, pricing, team and follow-up. We do not guarantee revenue or appointments.

Is WhatsApp, email, calendar or CRM already connected?

No. Any third-party connection requires supported accounts, permissions, testing and agreement on scope. The demo is self-contained and does not send messages.

Do you replace our sales staff?

No. The aim is to make qualification and human handoff more consistent, not to misrepresent automation as human communication.

What happens after I request a demo?

Your request is recorded for review. A response and appointment are not automatic, and no payment is taken through this form.
