How the calculation works

Additional timely responses = monthly enquiries × max(0, scenario response rate − current response rate). Additional opportunities = additional responses × opportunity rate. Additional sales = opportunities × close rate. Illustrative contribution = additional sales × average contribution per sale.

If the scenario response rate is lower than your current rate, the calculator shows zero incremental upside rather than presenting a negative change as an opportunity.

Measure your baseline before estimating

  1. Define a legitimate new enquiry and remove spam, duplicates and existing support cases.
  2. Choose a realistic response-time target for each channel; urgent safety matters require human escalation.
  3. Record the received time, first meaningful response time, assigned owner, qualified status and final outcome.
  4. Calculate response and conversion rates over a defined period. Check that your team can fulfil any additional work.
  5. Review results weekly, and compare like-for-like enquiry sources before attributing changes to a new process.

Important limitations

A faster response alone does not establish causation or create demand. Lead quality, pricing, seasonal demand, staff capacity and customer preferences affect conversion. No third-party integrations, WhatsApp sending or automated appointments are demonstrated by this tool. It collects no input values and makes no network requests.
