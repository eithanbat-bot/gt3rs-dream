Suggested first actions

    This is a descriptive self-check, not a validated benchmark, financial forecast or guarantee of more sales. Verify improvements with your own baseline data.

    What to measure after making a change

    Start with four operational measures: the share of enquiries assigned an owner, median time to first meaningful human response, share with a documented next action, and the number of opt-outs respected. Compare equivalent time periods and review whether the team can sustain the process before introducing more automation.
