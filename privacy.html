Privacy notice

LeadLift Revenue Systems ("LeadLift") is a developing service offer focused on lead-handling workflows for South African businesses. This notice explains how website enquiries and LeadLift account registration details are used. It is not a claim of certification or regulatory approval.

Information collected

When you submit a demo request, we collect the business and contact details you enter, your industry, approximate enquiry volume, any process description or challenge you provide, the submission timestamp, and your affirmative consent to be contacted about the specific request. Basic anti-abuse controls retain short-lived hashed connection identifiers and submission timestamps. Please avoid including passwords or sensitive customer records in the form.

Purpose and lawful basis

We use your submission only to review and respond to your request, assess fit, maintain related sales administration, prevent abuse and meet applicable requirements. The demo form is not a subscription to newsletters or unrelated marketing. We do not sell submitted contact details or infer consent from silence.

Where information is hosted

Website form submissions are held in a protected Supabase database hosted in the EU (Ireland region). Platform service providers may process technical connection information to deliver the website and protect against abuse. International data transfers must meet applicable legal requirements. No external messaging or payment provider is connected by this form.

Who can access information

The public website cannot read submitted form records. Access to the database is reserved for authorized operations and administrators. We do not publish customer enquiries, testimonials or prospect contact data.

LeadLift Inbox customer workspaces

Customers who sign up for the Inbox service provide an email and business name and configure their approved website domain. The system uses Supabase authentication and separate owner-only workspace records. It may hold customer enquiries submitted on each business's site (name, email, optional phone, message, status, consent and notes), access and expiry information, and support requests. This software does not send messages or invite unrelated marketing without a separate authorization. The business deploying the widget must provide its own privacy notice and meet its responsibilities for the enquiries it receives.

LeadLift Mail accounts, verification and signup IP

When you register for LeadLift Mail, we create a Supabase authentication user ID linked to your email address, record the registration and verification times, and create an account profile for possible future subscriptions. On registration our server also records the network IP address supplied by its gateway, where available, and the source of that network header. Proxy headers can be inaccurate and IP addresses are not proof of identity. This information is used for account administration, abuse detection and security, not for advertising, personal profiling or marketing. Password handling is performed by Supabase Auth, and passwords are not stored in the LeadLift profile database.

Signup IP addresses are restricted to server-side administrative access, are not shown in the customer application and are scheduled for deletion within 90 days (daily cleanup after expiry). A user ID and account verification record may be retained for the life of the account and applicable legal obligations. Subscription status defaults to not subscribed; signup does not authorize a payment or subscribe you to marketing. Email-verification messages are delivered by the configured Supabase Auth email service. Email delivery must be configured before unrestricted public signup can be offered.

Payment processing and campaign attribution

Where an authorized payment merchant is connected, checkout is hosted by the provider; card data must not be entered on this site. A verified successful payment may activate a customer account. At present merchant checkout is not enabled. When a visitor submits a demo request, limited UTM campaign labels from the URL may be saved with the consented request to understand which marketing channel generated it; visits without a submission are not recorded by this form. We do not run advertising tracking pixels.

Retention and your rights

We retain enquiries for as long as reasonably necessary to respond and administer the resulting business process, subject to applicable law. You may request access, correction, deletion where applicable, or object to processing. Please use the demo-request form and begin the challenge field with "PRIVACY REQUEST" (provide minimal identifying information). Such requests are handled separately from sales interest. Do not include government IDs or sensitive records in the public form.

Cookies and analytics

This site does not currently operate an advertising tracking pixel or third-party behavioral analytics script. Hosting infrastructure may process ordinary service logs. The interactive sample demo runs locally in your browser and does not save your sample message.

Contact

For a privacy request or questions about processing, reach the service operator through the contact form and label the request as such. The commercial operator's registered legal details are not published here and must be supplied before entering a paid service agreement.
