Customer handover & user guide

This guide is for customers who have purchased LeadLift Inbox and whose workspace is activated following verified payment.

1. Access your private workspace

Open the customer portal and sign in using your verified email. After a successful payment event, your business workspace changes from pending_payment to active. A browser redirect alone cannot activate it.

2. Embed the form

Copy the script from your active portal and paste it into your company website's page HTML, where your enquiries should appear. The form accepts submissions only from the verified HTTPS website domain saved when the workspace was created. If you change domains, request assistance before moving the form.

3. Test before relying on it

  1. Open the form from your approved website (not a local file or unrelated domain).
  2. Submit a test enquiry with a unique test email and explicit contact consent.
  3. Log into your portal and confirm the enquiry appears.
  4. Change its status and save a note; reload to confirm persistence.
  5. Remove the test enquiry in your export history if required by your data policy; contact support for a deletion request if the interface does not expose one.

4. Manage incoming enquiries

Each enquiry shows a name, email, message, urgency flag and status. Choose new, qualified, contacted, appointment, won or lost. Notes stay in your private workspace. The system does not send messages or schedule calls: your staff must complete and verify those steps.

5. Download your data

Use Export CSV to download the currently loaded enquiry records. Keep exported files private and delete copies you no longer need.

6. Support and ownership

Open a support ticket from your active portal if the widget fails, if you need help changing domain, or for defects in the LeadLift software. Do not put passwords or customer identity documents into a ticket. No guaranteed response SLA is included unless explicitly agreed in a signed contract.

Package limits

One business owner login, one approved HTTPS domain, up to 250 saved leads per rolling 30 days, and six months of access from activation. Renewals are quoted separately. WhatsApp, SMS, email sending, calendars and AI models are not part of this package.

7. Your privacy obligations

Your business is responsible for appropriate customer notices, lawful grounds for processing, keeping contact information secure, handling opt-outs and ensuring only authorized staff can access exported records. LeadLift hosts the portal as a technical service; the commercial agreement must name the responsible operator and data processing terms.
